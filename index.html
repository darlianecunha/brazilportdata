About

Brazil Port Data is a research platform that connects international stakeholders with structured, reliable data on the Brazilian port sector, now paired with a European maritime observatory. It supports port authorities, investors, and maritime organisations in understanding infrastructure, performance, and sustainability across ports.

Leadership

Darliane Cunha, PhD leads the Brazil Port Data initiative. She holds a doctorate in Finance and Accounting (Universidad de Zaragoza, Spain) and completed a post-doctorate at Erasmus University Rotterdam, with more than twenty years of research in maritime sustainability and port governance. Her work spans emissions monitoring, SDG implementation, and data analytics for the maritime sector, bridging academic rigour and applied policy insight.
